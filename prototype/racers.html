What you're seeing

A full top-down track, arcade kart physics, drift, boost strips, lap & position tracking, and a live race board — exactly the HUD real multiplayer would use.

What multiplayer adds

Swap the three bots for real players over the network. Same screen, same board — each rival's kart is driven by a friend instead of the computer.

Why it feels smooth

Your kart always moves instantly on your screen; the network quietly reconciles the rivals. That's how ~80ms of real lag ends up feeling like none.

Prototype for ZipZap Games. Rival karts are AI standing in for networked players. Real-time sync would run on a game server (WebSocket/WebRTC) rather than the setup the word games use.
