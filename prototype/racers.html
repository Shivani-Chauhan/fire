Real multiplayer

Create a room, share the 4-letter code, and everyone drives on the same track live. Each kart is a real player — your kart moves instantly, rivals are smoothed over the network.

Three tracks

Sunset Loop, Speedway, and Twister — the host picks in the lobby. Each has its own flow, corners, and boost strips.

Weapons

Drive through item boxes for a random pickup: boost, a homing missile, or a mine to drop behind you. Get hit and you spin out.

Prototype for ZipZap Games. Online play uses Firebase (the same backend as the other games). If the room screen says online is unavailable, it's because this preview can't reach Firebase — it works once the file is hosted on your site.
